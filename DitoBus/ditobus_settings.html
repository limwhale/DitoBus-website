Help & Support

Bus Tracking & Routes

Q: How do I view live bus locations?

A: Click on the "Maps" link in the navigation header to open the interactive map of active DitoBus routes.

Q: Why is the map restricted to a specific area?

A: The map boundaries are locked to the designated operational coverage zone to ensure accurate tracking.

Q: How do I check the bus schedules?

A: Click on the "Bus Schedule" link in the header navigation to view upcoming arrival and departure times.

About Us

DitoBus is a centralized transit monitoring and schedule tracking web application built for Nueva Ecija commuter operations, supporting real-time employee shifts and interactive route visualization.

What You Can Do

Live Bus Map

See active buses moving along their routes on the map.

Bus Schedule

Check departure times, estimated arrivals, and whether a bus is scheduled, en route, or arrived.

Conductor Shifts

Conductors can register a shift so their bus shows up on the map and the schedule.

Personalization

Switch between light and dark mode and choose your own bus icon and route line colors.

Where We Operate

DitoBus covers terminals in Cabanatuan, San Jose, Gapan, Muñoz, Guimba, and Talavera, with routes reaching as far as Palayan, Rizal, and Pantabangan.
